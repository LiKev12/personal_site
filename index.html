Avalon

A hobby project built with a friend — a full online version of the social-deduction board game The Resistance: Avalon, so fans can play together over the web with hidden roles, live chat, and competitive matchmaking.

rules live site source
Built with

React · Node/Express · MongoDB · Socket.IO (real-time gameplay & chat) · Reactstrap · Recharts

Features
  • Private & public games — password-gated private rooms and open public rooms, all visible in the game lobby; spectators can watch either (with the password for private games).
  • Rated & unrated games — a ranked lobby where every player starts at 1500 ELO; each team's average rating drives per-player gains and losses, and abandoning a game dings everyone's rating.
  • In-game utilities — a full game transcript, a voting record of every proposed team, and player chat in each room.
  • Individual stats — game history plus win/loss splits by team and by role, including head-to-head.
  • App stats — player and user growth over time, and a top-5 leaderboard by rating.
How to play
  1. Register a user (username, email, password).
  2. Create a game in the lobby and join the room.
  3. Wait for others to join — or invite your friends.
  4. Pick the roles and setup for your game.
  5. Play!
FAQ

How do I play Avalon?

See the in-app Rules page, or the written rules (PDF) and a video explanation.

How is the game-room leader determined?

The first person to enter the room — not necessarily whoever created it.

Are there restrictions on a valid game setup?

Yes: 5–12 players; at least one role from each team; more than half the players on the Resistance team; Percival and Morgana must be included together; and Drunk Merlin only if Merlin is also in.

What does the Drunk Merlin role do?

Short answer: cause chaos. Drunk Merlin sees himself as Merlin with Merlin-style information — except it's completely random, so he may "see" Resistance players as Spies. It also makes the real Merlin less certain of his own role, since he could be the Drunk one.

Who assassinates if the Assassin role isn't in the game?

Precedence goes Assassin → Morgana → Minion → Mordred → Oberon. In practice the Spy team should agree on a target together first.

Is there a time limit?

Yes — a hard 4-hour limit, after which a game is finished and deleted.

I accidentally closed my browser — can I rejoin?

Yes, log back in and rejoin the game room.

How does the ELO rating work?

Higher rating = higher expected skill. If a stronger team is favored to win, they gain less for winning (and the underdog gains more). Winners always gain ELO and losers always lose it.

Can I play on mobile?

It wasn't designed for mobile — use it as a web app for the best experience.
